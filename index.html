Starfield

Relativistic Visualizer and Simulator

Accelerate a spacecraft from everyday speeds to 0.99999c and observe the consequences: starlight aberrates forward, colors Doppler-shift, clocks and rulers diverge.

Begin Simulation About the project

Aberration

Starlight crowds into the direction of travel. The sky ahead tightens while the space behind you empties out.

Doppler Shift

Stars you are racing toward shift blue. Whilst the ones falling behind shift red.

Clocks & Rulers

Compare Earth's clock against your own, Earth's measured distance against the length-contracted one.
