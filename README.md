# Starfield

**A relativity explorer.**

An interactive, browser-based visualization of relativistic space travel. Pick a destination, push the throttle toward the speed of light, and watch the sky transform in real time as aberration sweeps the stars forward and Doppler shift bleeds their colors toward blue and red. A live HUD reports the physics behind the view: velocity, Lorentz factor (γ), time dilation, length contraction, and the widening gap between the Earth clock and the traveler's own.

## Project

This project is an interactive educational visualization of Einstein's Special Theory of Relativity. It aims to help students intuitively explore concepts such as relativistic velocity, time dilation, length contraction, aberration of light, and Doppler shifting through real-time visual simulation.
